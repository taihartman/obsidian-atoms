# Atoms voice

## Mandatory

1. **Read** [`docs/voice.md`](../../../docs/voice.md) before drafting or critiquing copy.
2. **Obey hard rules** there: no em dashes, no guilt/task-app language, no “use case” marketing, body sacred.
3. **Field notes** = second brain in real life + how others run it + invite their practice. Not a changelog drip.

## When drafting Field notes / marketing email

- One concrete beat first (walk, person, almost-forgotten moment).
- Then the stack only as it serves that beat (Obsidian, Claude, Atoms).
- Soft CTA + invite to reply with their practice.
- Under-promise cadence; prefer silence to filler.
- CAN-SPAM: unsubscribe + physical address (ops: `docs/runbooks/atoms-notes-list.md`).

## When editing existing copy

- Match surrounding product voice (landing hero and home are the gold standard).
- Prefer cut over clever. Specific over abstract.
- Positivity without corn: relief and possibility, not pep talk.

## Output

- Deliver final copy ready to paste (email body, subject, or UI string).
- If something violates `docs/voice.md`, say so and fix it - do not ship “close enough.”
- Do not invent pricing/trial claims; use SSOT.

## Out of scope

- Rewriting user capture or atom bodies.
- Visual design (use design-handoff tokens / frontend skills).
- Choosing Resend segment IDs or secrets (runbook).
