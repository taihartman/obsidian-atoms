## The two things a QA run must actually prove

1. **Sign out disarms the mirror and clears device state.** Settings shows the mirror off; the
   egress sentinel logs nothing across an account switch — **paired with a positive control in
   the same session**, because an empty log and a broken probe are byte-identical.
2. **A sync already in flight cannot un-do that.** This is the subtle one and it is the reason
   the diff grew past two files. Sign out *while a sync is running*, then confirm the baseline
   stays cleared after the pass settles.

Recipe, evidence rules, and the Blocked convention are in the plan's **Verification contract**:
`docs/plans/2026-08-09-001-fix-372-sign-out-teardown-plan.md`.

  popout `dev:screenshot` cannot see.
- **`dev:screenshot` can return the frame from before your last re-render.** Capture twice, keep
  only byte-identical pairs, then look at the images. `docs/qa/app-navigation-map.md`.

  occupies the same `plusBaseUrl` override a sign-in would need. Seed the second identity as a

  `docs/qa/2026-08-08-371-374-mirror-consent-truth-world-class-qa.md`.

## What was already decided — do not re-litigate

- **KTD1 (owner-directed):** sign-out disarms and clears outright; the account-keyed baseline
  that would preserve the same-account fast path is #396, not a blocker.
- **The acks survive sign-out on purpose.** Withdrawal keys off the ack timestamp, so clearing
  them would delete the only route back out. A consequence the owner accepted: re-arming after
  an account switch is a bare toggle, not a fresh consent sheet.
- **`askEnabled` is vault-synced**, so sign-out turns the mirror off on *every* device sharing
  the vault. Accepted; the row copy was rewritten to say so.
- **No try/catch around `signOutPlus`** — it returns errors as values and cannot throw
  (`plusRequest` catches at `plusClient.ts:214`). A wrapper would be dead code. Two tests pin
  this instead.
