## Task 2 — the power-user story on tryatoms.app (after #168)

Queued deliberately behind #168, because the story's whole premise is *depth*,
and catch-up is what creates depth on day one. Building it first means writing
"wait a year" and then rewriting it.

**The gap it closes.** All three existing storylines (Relationships / Work /
Journaling) have the same shape: one question, one atom, one quote. That proves
retrieval. It does **not** prove the graph, which is the actual thesis. The
`#graph` section is currently decorative — nothing on the page shows a link being
*used* to reach an answer a single note could not give.

**Shape agreed with the repo owner:**

- **Not a fourth carousel tab.** The three are peer life domains ("pick the one
  that's like you"). A power-user scenario is a different *tier*, not a fourth
  domain, and slotting it alongside implies a new user gets this on day one. Give
  it its own section, positioned as "and after a year of this."
- **The strongest version is the three storylines converging.** A decision
  question answerable only by joining across all three — the worked example was
  *"should I take the Denver offer?"* pulling the partner's job constraint, last
  winter's note about hating the cold, the friend who already lives there, and
  wanting to be closer to climbing. Four citations, four dates, four domains. The
  beat: **your past self answers a hard question**, which is a far bigger claim
  than "it found your note."
- **Honesty constraint, held hard.** Ask does literal keyword and tag matching
  plus link traversal — not semantic inference. Every line of the answer must be
  traceable to a quoted atom, and the conclusion must read as an obvious *join*,
  never a leap the model invented. If it looks like magic, it is a lie about the
  product.
