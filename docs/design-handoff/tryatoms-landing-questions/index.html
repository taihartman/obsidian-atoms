Questions

A few things people ask.

Can I just use Cowork on my vault?

Yes. Cowork is fine. Atoms is the part before you ask. A thought is added to Obsidian in a few seconds, then titled and linked. After that you can ask from Cowork, or from Claude or ChatGPT with the connector. The connector is built for those filed notes. It does not need Cowork running, and it does not need the vault on the machine you are typing on.

What about pictures?

Capture is type or voice. The iPhone shortcut and Atoms Capture do not take photos, and Atoms does not read the picture. If a picture is already in your vault, it stays. If you wrote a line about it, that line is what gets filed.

How does this help with quick capture?

Quick capture is the first step. Finding is what happens after. Shortcut on iPhone, Atoms Capture on Android. Type or talk. Your thought is added to Obsidian in a few seconds. You can open the daily later and add to it. Then it gets processed and linked.
