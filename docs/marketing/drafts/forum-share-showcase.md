**Title:** New plugin: Atoms. Files your past daily captures into linked atomic notes. Your words stay verbatim.

**Body:**

Hi all. I built Atoms because my daily notes were where thoughts went to disappear. I capture fast, on walks, in five seconds. I never went back to file any of it.

Atoms goes through your past daily notes and files each capture as its own small note: a clear title, tags, and links with a stated reason. The body of every atom is your capture text, word for word. The model never rewrites your thoughts. It only writes the title, tags, and links. I care about this more than any other part: a confident paraphrase of a half-formed thought is a lie about what you knew at the time.

What it deliberately does not do:

- It never touches today's daily note. Mid-day capture stays quiet.
- It never moves files or invents folders. Atoms land flat in one folder. The intelligence is in links and titles.
- It is not a task app. Errands classify as noise and stay out of the library. No due dates, no streaks, no backlog count.
- Nothing is destroyed. It only creates atom files and appends small markers under processed captures.

There is a home view with a gentle resurfacing stream (not a review queue; empty can be correct), person hubs, an atom graph filtered to your atoms, and optionally a way to ask Claude or ChatGPT about your atoms through a mirror of the flat atoms folder. Your vault stays the source of truth.

Honesty about costs: filing uses a model, so it needs either your own Anthropic API key (Anthropic bills you for usage; the plugin is MIT and free) or Atoms Plus, a hosted subscription for people who do not want to handle a key. Resurfacing, home, hubs, and the graph are free either way and stay that way. What each run sends is documented plainly: note titles, tags, person-hub titles, and the capture text, over TLS. Full details: [what leaves your machine page, once live]

Install from the community directory (search "Atoms", author Tai Hartman) or read the from-zero guide at tryatoms.app/setup. Capture on phones: an iOS Shortcut, and a small Android capture app on Google Play.

I would genuinely like to hear how you run daily capture, and where Atoms files something badly. Bad classifications are never lossy, so worst case you delete a small file.
